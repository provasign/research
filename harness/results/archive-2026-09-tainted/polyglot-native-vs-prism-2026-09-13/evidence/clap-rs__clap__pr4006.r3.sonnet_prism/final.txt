## Summary

The bug: when an arg required via `requires()` was also a member of an already-satisfied `ArgGroup`, `Usage::get_required_usage_from` filtered it out of the missing-required-args list entirely (since group members are normally omitted because the group's own usage notation, e.g. `<--first|--second>`, already covers them). This produced an empty header even though the error still fired.

Fix in `src/parser/validator.rs` (`Validator::missing_required_error`): after computing the usual `req_args`, add back any argument from `self.required` (or explicitly `incl`uded) that is a plain `Arg` (not a group), still missing, and not already listed — covering the case where an arg is required independently of its group membership.

Verified:
- The reported repro now produces the expected error header (`--first`) with an unchanged usage line.
