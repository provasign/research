## Summary

**Root cause**: `Replacer::replace_all` in `crates/printer/src/util.rs` handled multi-line replacements by extending the searched `subject` slice past `range.end` with extra look-ahead bytes (`MAX_LOOK_AHEAD`) so regex look-around could see beyond the current match. It then delegated to `Matcher::replace_with_captures_at`, which iterates *all* matches in that extended subject and, at the end, blindly copies everything from the last match to the end of `subject` into the output. Since `subject` included the look-ahead bytes, this trailing copy leaked unrelated content (and any additional matches found in the look-ahead region) into the replacement output.

**Fix**: Replaced the call to `replace_with_captures_at` with a manual loop using `captures_iter_at` that:
- keeps replacing all matches whose start is within `range` (preserving existing multi-match-per-line behavior),
- stops as soon as a match starts at or beyond `range.end` (i.e., it only exists because of the look-ahead bytes), and
- bounds the final trailing unmatched-byte copy to `range.end` instead of the full (look-ahead-extended) `subject`.

This is scoped to `crates/printer/src/util.rs` only.
