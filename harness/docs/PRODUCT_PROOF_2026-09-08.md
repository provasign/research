# Prism product proof — 2026-09-08

## Decision

The current product has strong evidence for change-impact work, especially with
Sonnet. It has only a weak coding-correctness signal with materially worse
efficiency. The evidence supports a narrow change-impact claim, not a claim
that Prism improves average day-to-day coding.

The evaluated Prism binary was built from `c805a1101951e03a657170fb92c000254dd64a74`
and had SHA-256
`5738830504e8deaad15d7f4c8333783ff95dd233dd0e5d72d2a2fb53e8d00aba`.
Both suites used Claude Sonnet 5 and GPT-5.5 at medium effort, native versus
Prism, with byte-identical task prompts and normal tool choice. Prism arms used
the product files generated by `prism init --no-permissions`; native arms did
not contain them. There were no quality retries.

The initial two-task coding screen did not meet the predetermined expansion
criterion. The remaining tasks were run afterward at the user's explicit
request and are labeled as a user-authorized expansion, not a passed gate.

## Change-impact: 36 cells

Evidence:

- `runs/product-proof-impact-pilot-2026-09-08`
- `runs/product-proof-impact-remaining-2026-09-08`

Nine pinned tasks, four arms, one trial produced:

| Treatment | Valid cells | Mean recall | Mean precision | Mean F1 | Exact-perfect | Total wall time | Known cost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Native | 16/18 | 0.8352 | 0.7871 | 0.7824 | 5/18 | 3,108.271 s | $13.741409 across 16/18 |
| Prism | 18/18 | 0.9420 | 0.9448 | 0.9094 | 9/18 | 1,589.924 s | $5.368141 across 18/18 |

Prism raised exact-perfect answers from five to nine, increased mean F1 by 0.1270,
and used about half the wall time. Its complete cost was lower than even the
incomplete native cost total. Every Prism impact cell adopted Prism.

The effect is model-dependent:

| Model | Native mean F1 | Prism mean F1 | Native exact | Prism exact |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Sonnet | 0.6589 | 0.9925 | 1/9 | 5/9 |
| GPT-5.5 | 0.9059 | 0.8262 | 4/9 | 4/9 |

Sonnet Prism completed all nine cells; Sonnet native timed out twice. GPT Prism
was cheaper and faster overall but had important quality failures: it
over-reported Jackson `JsonNode.get` (precision 0.6667) and returned only one
of the Guava `ForwardingObject.delegate` sites (recall 0.0031). The latter was
a genuine successful CLI Prism call, not missing integration.

This supports a product-level change-impact benefit overall and a particularly
strong Sonnet result. It does not support a GPT-5.5 impact-quality claim without
addressing the two failure modes.

## Day-to-day coding: 32 cells

Evidence:

- `runs/product-proof-coding-pilot-fixed-2026-09-08`
- `runs/product-proof-coding-remaining-2026-09-08`

Eight pinned tasks covered two local bugs, two cross-file fixes, two small
features, and two maintenance/refactoring tasks. Every patch, including the one
timeout patch, was scored with held-out Docker `FAIL_TO_PASS` and
`PASS_TO_PASS` tests.

| Treatment | Valid measurements | Correct fixes | Regressions | Total wall time | Known cost | Time/correct | Known cost/correct |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Native | 15/16 | 4/16 | 2 | 1,665.144 s | $4.307581 across 15/16 | 416.286 s | at least $1.076895 |
| Prism | 16/16 | 5/16 | 2 | 2,157.586 s | $8.500949 across 16/16 | 431.517 s | $1.700190 |

Prism produced one additional correct fix at the same per-cell budget, entirely
in the GPT-5.5 stratum:

| Model | Native correct | Prism correct | Native regressions | Prism regressions | Prism adoption |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Sonnet | 2/8 | 2/8 | 0 | 0 | 1/8 cells |
| GPT-5.5 | 2/8 | 3/8 | 2 | 2 | 8/8 cells |

The unique Prism success was GPT-5.5 on Click compatibility (`pr3466`), where
the other three arms missed the target. Correctness was otherwise concentrated
in the easy Click `pr3244` and Rich `pr3882` tasks. All four arms failed both
cross-file tasks, the Click feature, the Click refactor, and Werkzeug.

All four Click-refactor candidate patches caused held-out-test collection
failures. The original and reference patch had passed the preflight oracle, so
these are scored candidate failures. Werkzeug remains interpretation-sensitive:
its prompt/reference alignment had been questioned before this run, and all
four arms failed it; it should be reported separately in any published table.

Current Prism therefore shows a small coding-correctness gain (5 versus 4) but
does not show an efficiency gain: total wall time rose 29.6%, known spend nearly
doubled, and time per correct fix rose 3.7%. One trial over eight tasks is also
too small for a broad average-coding claim.

## Invalidated predecessor

`runs/product-proof-coding-pilot-2026-09-08` remains invalid. Dependency
installation generated `src/urllib3/_version.py` in the native template after
the Prism template had been copied. The corrected runner prepares dependencies
first, copies that exact tree into the Prism template, installs Prism second,
and aborts if any base file differs.

## Steering attribution

These product suites compare current Prism with native operation. They establish
the behavior of the product containing steering commit `c37a1f4`; they do not
isolate that commit causally. The earlier single GPT impact pair favored the new
steering, but a proper old-versus-new steering attribution panel remains a
separate experiment.
